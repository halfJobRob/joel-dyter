## When you send an enquiry

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, the postcode of the property, the type of project, a rough budget and timescale, a description of what you have in mind, and any photos or drawings you choose to attach. Only your name, email, the type of project and the description are required.

These details are used to reply to you, talk through your project, prepare an estimate and, if you go ahead, carry out the work. You ask to be contacted when you send the form, and that is the basis on which your details are used.

Photos are usually reduced in size in your browser before they are sent, which also removes hidden information such as the location where a photo was taken.

The form is handled by the company that hosts this website and arrives as an email in Joel's business mailbox. Enquiries are not saved on the website itself.

## When you call, email or send a WhatsApp message

Messages and calls are handled by your phone or email provider in the usual way. WhatsApp messages are also handled by WhatsApp, under its own privacy policy.

## Website statistics and cookies

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics to see how people find the site and which pages they look at. It records things like the pages visited, how you arrived (for example, from a Google search), the type of device and browser, and your approximate area. It does not tell Joel who you are.

Google Analytics only runs if you click **Accept** on the message that appears on your first visit. It then sets two cookies, `_ga` and `_ga_MZBNJF71Y6`. If you click **No thanks**, no statistics cookies are set.

Your choice is remembered in your browser so you are not asked on every page. This site uses no other cookies.

## Who your information is shared with

Your details are never sold, and never used by anyone else for marketing. A few services help run the website and handle information on Joel's behalf:

- **Armour Hosting**, which hosts the website and sends on the enquiry form
- **123-reg**, which provides Joel's email
- **Google**, for website statistics, only if you have accepted them

Like most websites, the hosting company also keeps standard technical records, such as the internet address of each visitor and the pages requested, for security and for a limited time.

## How long your information is kept

Your details are kept only for as long as they are needed to deal with your enquiry or project, and afterwards only where the law requires it, for example for tax and accounting records.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. You can also change your mind about website statistics at any time using the button above. To do any of these, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.
