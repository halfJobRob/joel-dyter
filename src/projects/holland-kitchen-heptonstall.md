The owner bought the cottage while still living in Dorset, so Joel took on the whole renovation: lifting the lintel to set the Rayburn three feet back into the chimney breast, replacing the quarry tiles with stone, and making and fitting the kitchen. Photos and progress notes went to the owner throughout.

Joel has since made doors and windows for the same house.
