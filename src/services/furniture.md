Some of my favourite work is a single piece: a dining table long enough for the whole family, a desk with a curved front and drawers that follow the curve, or a full-width panelled headboard.

Each piece is designed with you, made by hand in the workshop and finished to suit its room. Every piece leaves with my maker's plate on it.

I also make furniture for businesses, such as office desks, meeting tables and storage. [See the office furniture project](/projects/office-furniture/).
