A handmade kitchen should outlast the house's next three owners. That comes down to two things: the materials, and the time spent getting it right.

I start with a visit and a conversation. How do you cook? Where does everyone end up standing? What has to stay, such as the range, the beams or that alcove? From there I draw the room by hand, so you can see the layout, proportions and details before a single board is cut.

Everything is then made in my workshop in Hebden Bridge: painted or natural timber doors, solid carcasses, hand-dovetailed drawers, plate racks, larders and islands. When it's ready, I fit it myself, scribing each piece to your walls until it looks as though it was built with the house.
