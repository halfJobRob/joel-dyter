In many Calder Valley houses, standard windows and doors simply don't fit.

Old stone openings are rarely square, and seldom a standard size. Timber windows and doors made to measure fit properly, keep the weather out and ***respect the character of the building, inside and out***.

Joel also takes on finer joinery, including panelling, glazed screens and floors with inlaid detail, ***so the craftsmanship carries on once you step through the door***.
