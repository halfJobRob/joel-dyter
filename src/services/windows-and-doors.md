Many houses in the Calder Valley have stone openings that no standard window or door will fit. I make timber windows, front doors and internal doors to measure, built from solid timber and fitted by me.

Alongside windows and doors I take on fine joinery work, from panelling and glazed screens to floors with inlaid detail.
