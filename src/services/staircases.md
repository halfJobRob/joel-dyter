A staircase is often the first thing you see when you come through the door.

It's also the piece of joinery you use most, every morning and every night, so it needs to be strong, safe and comfortable underfoot. Designed properly, it carries the character of your home from one floor to the next, so ***each room flows naturally into the next***.

Joel sets out and makes each staircase in the workshop, designed around the house it is going into, and fits it himself. Where there's room beneath, ***storage can be built in so no space is wasted***.
