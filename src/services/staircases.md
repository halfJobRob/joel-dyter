A staircase is the piece of joinery you use most and notice first. It has to be strong, safe and comfortable to climb, and it should look as if it belongs in the house.

I set out and make staircases in the workshop: traditional oak stairs, open-tread designs and spiral stairs. Each is built from site measurements and fitted by me. Where there's space below, I can build storage into it so nothing is wasted.
