A staircase is often the first thing you see when you come through the door, and the piece of joinery you use most. It needs to be strong, safe and comfortable underfoot, and it should suit the character of the house.

Joel sets out and makes staircases in the workshop, from traditional oak stairs to open-tread and spiral designs, and fits them himself. Where there is room beneath, storage can be built in so the space is not wasted.
